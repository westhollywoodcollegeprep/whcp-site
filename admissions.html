Admissions & Enrollment

Enrollment is open all year round. We welcome families from all backgrounds and offer programs for every stage of learning.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What ages and grades does WHCP serve?

WHCP serves Pre‑school (ages 2–4) and Kindergarten (age 5) in our Early Childhood program, Elementary School for Grades 1–5, and Middle & High School for Grades 6–12, all on one campus in West Hollywood.

Is WHCP accredited?

Yes. West Hollywood College Preparatory School is accredited by the Western Association of Schools and Colleges (WASC).

Is the preschool licensed?

Yes. Our early childhood program is a Licensed Childcare Center.

When can we apply?

Enrollment is open all year round. Contact us, visit the campus, submit an application, and complete enrollment paperwork once your child is accepted.

How much is tuition?

For 2026–2027, monthly tuition is $1,600 for 2 Years–Kindergarten, $1,700 for Grades 1–5, $1,900 for Grades 6–8, $2,000 for Grade 9, and $2,300 for Grades 10–12. Annual one-payment options and a siblings discount are available; see the tuition table above.

How big are classes?

Classes are intentionally small, so every student is known, challenged, and supported. Early childhood groups are kept very small so each child receives individual attention.

Do you offer after-school care?

Yes. Our After School Program runs from the end of the school day until 5:00 PM and includes supervised study, enrichment, and childcare. Summer School, Summer Camp, and Saturday School are also available.

Can international students enroll?

Yes. Students in Grades K–12 from any country are welcome year-round. WHCP is SEVP-certified to enroll F-1 students and helps families with I-20 documentation.

Do you offer tutoring for students who don't attend WHCP?

Our Tutoring Center offers one-on-one and small-group sessions in all core subjects, plus SAT and ACT preparation, after school, on weekends, and during school breaks. Contact us to discuss your student's needs.

How do we schedule a tour?

Call (323) 822-7900 or send us a message through our contact form. Our office is open Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, at 1317 N. Crescent Heights Blvd., West Hollywood, CA 90046.
