Transcripts & Diplomas

How former students request an official transcript or their diploma.

Transcripts

Requesting a Transcript

For a School or Employer

The college, university, trade school, or employer emails the official transcript request to info@westhollywoodschool.com. There is no cost to you.

For Personal Use

Request your own copy by mail for $50, following the steps below.

Personal copies are mailed only. They cannot be picked up in person and are only sent to the mailing address in your letter.

Send

A signed and dated letter asking for a copy of your transcript, mailed to 1317 N. Crescent Heights Blvd., West Hollywood, CA 90046.

Include

Your full name, date of birth, mailing address, and phone number.

Fee

Also, include a $50 money order or cashier’s check made payable to “West Hollywood College Preparatory School”. No personal checks, cash, or other payment.

Turnaround

Up to 5 business days after we receive your request. Your transcript is mailed in a sealed envelope.

Diplomas

Requesting Your Diploma

Diplomas are mailed only. They cannot be picked up in person and are only sent to the graduate’s mailing address.

Only the graduate can request it. Friends, relatives, parents, schools, and employers cannot request a diploma on the graduate’s behalf.

Send

A signed and dated letter asking for your diploma, mailed to 1317 N. Crescent Heights Blvd., West Hollywood, CA 90046.

Include

Your full name, date of birth, mailing address, and phone number.

Fee

Also, include a $100 money order or cashier’s check made payable to “West Hollywood College Preparatory School”. No personal checks, cash, or other payment.

Turnaround

Up to 5 business days after we receive your request. Your diploma is mailed to the address in your letter.

Questions? Email info@westhollywoodschool.com or call (323) 822-7900.
