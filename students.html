Student Life

Activities, leadership, community service, and academic support for every WHCP student.

Enrichment

Student Activities

Students are encouraged to participate in after-school and extracurricular activities for their own enrichment and to strengthen their college applications. Schoolwork always comes first — activities are supported by a faculty or parent advisor once enough students express interest.

Activities Available

Depending on student interest, activities may include Student Government, class trips, school fundraisers, local election campaigns, the Student Art Show, and Chess.

Field Trips

Educational trips supplement classroom learning several times per year. Students and parents may suggest and help plan trips. Past destinations include the Natural History Museum, the Getty Center, the Skirball Cultural Center, West Hollywood City Hall, the Museum of Latin American Art, and theater outings.

Leadership

Student Government

Middle and High School students in grades 6–12 can serve as student body leaders in WHCP's Student Government, hosting activities and fundraisers throughout the year under a faculty advisor.

Past activities include a bake sale for Breast Cancer Awareness, the Halloween Potluck Party, the Thanksgiving Celebration & Show, and a bake sale benefiting the Lange Foundation animal shelter.

Giving Back

Community Service

WHCP requires students in grades 9–12 to complete 100 community service hours prior to graduation. Counselors and staff help identify opportunities suited to each student's interests and abilities, and keep records of participation. Parental support is essential.

Past Projects

West Hollywood Book Fair · Revlon Walk for Breast Cancer · AIDS Walk Los Angeles · West Hollywood Kids Fair · West Hollywood Russian Culture Festival · West Hollywood Holocaust Remembrance Day · Red Cross Hurricane Katrina Relief Fund.

Support

Academic Counseling

In WHCP's small school setting, teachers, counselors, and administrators team up to motivate students and help them be their best — academically, personally, and socially. We promote responsibility, self-discipline, strong ethics, and self-respect alongside rigorous college preparation.

Registration & Planning

Students are registered in classes based on ability and achievement, and assisted in building a long-range academic plan that prepares them for future training and work.
