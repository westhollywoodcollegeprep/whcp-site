Parent Resources

Information and opportunities for parents to stay engaged and involved in the WHCP community.

Get Involved

Parent Involvement

Parent involvement is essential to the success of students at WHCP. Parents are encouraged to take part in school life in a number of ways:

PTO Meetings

Participate in monthly Parent Teacher Organization meetings. The PTO helps organize school events and projects, leads fundraisers, and coordinates parents and teachers in these activities.

Field Trips

Assist with school field trips by driving children to destinations and acting as chaperones throughout the year.

Progress Meetings

Request student progress report meetings, available throughout the year, where parents meet with teachers to discuss their child's academic development.

Events & Projects

Participate in school projects and events throughout the year — helping organize, supplying resources, and attending are all great ways to be part of student life.

Activity Advisor

Become an advisor for after-school student activities such as Student Government, the Student Art Show, and school fundraisers, based on student interest.

Career Day

Come share what you do for work with our students at Career Day, helping them learn about different careers and pathways.

PTO

Parent Teacher Organization

WHCP's Parent Teacher Organization meets regularly to discuss ways for parents to enhance the quality of, and resources for, student life and learning. We invite you to become a PTO leader — organizing school projects, events, and fundraisers and bringing other parents and teachers on board to support your work.

To attend our next PTO meeting, please contact the school office.

Contact the School Office
Communication

Parent & Teacher Meetings

We encourage parents and teachers to communicate about their child's progress throughout the school year. Healthy parent–teacher partnerships are key to every student's success.

Meetings by Request

Parents may request a meeting with a teacher at any time through the Director. Teachers may also request meetings with parents, and families will be notified by the Director.
