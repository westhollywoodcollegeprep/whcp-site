Privacy Policy

How West Hollywood College Preparatory School collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective date: January 1, 2026

West Hollywood College Preparatory School (“WHCP,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to information collected through this site and is provided in keeping with the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and other applicable privacy laws.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you choose to provide it — primarily through our contact and admissions inquiry forms. Depending on the form, the categories of personally identifiable information we may collect include:

  • Identifiers such as your name and, for admissions inquiries, a prospective student’s name;
  • Contact details such as email address and telephone number;
  • The program of interest and any message or comments you submit; and
  • Limited technical information your browser sends automatically, such as general device or browser type.

We do not knowingly collect payment card numbers, government identification numbers, or sensitive personal information through this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, process admissions interest, schedule tours, communicate with you about our programs, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Categories of Third Parties

We may share information with service providers who help us operate this website and respond to inquiries — for example, our form-processing and email-delivery provider (Formspree, which receives the information you submit through our forms), and our website hosting provider (GitHub Pages). These providers act on our behalf and are not authorized to use your information for unrelated purposes. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our community.

Cookies & Do Not Track

This website does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not track visitors across other websites over time. Because we do not track you across third-party websites, we do not respond differently to “Do Not Track” (DNT) signals sent by your browser. We are not aware of third parties collecting personally identifiable information about your online activities across different websites when you visit this site.

Student & Children’s Privacy

Inquiry forms on this site are intended to be completed by parents, guardians, or prospective adult students. We are committed to protecting the privacy of children and students and handle any student information consistent with applicable laws, including the federal Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and California student-privacy laws. Please do not submit a child’s information through this site except as part of a parent- or guardian-initiated admissions inquiry.

How We Protect Your Information

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information submitted through this website. No method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Reviewing or Changing Your Information

You may request to review, update, or delete the personal information you have submitted through this website by contacting us using the details below. We will respond to your request consistent with applicable law.

Your California Privacy Rights

California residents may have rights regarding their personal information under California law. To exercise any available rights, or to ask how your information is handled, please contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will revise the effective date above and post the updated policy on this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.
