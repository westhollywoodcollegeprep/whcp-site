A School Where Every Student Is Known

West Hollywood College Preparatory School is a private, nonprofit Pre‑school through 12th grade center of learning located in the heart of West Hollywood, California. We emphasize achievement in math, language arts, and science.

Students learn in small classes with a strong curriculum and faculty, and are encouraged to take part in civic and community life. Our intimate class sizes mean every student is seen, challenged, and supported — from their first day of pre‑school to their college acceptance letter.

Our Mission & Goals

College-ready from the start

Rigorous academics aligned with university entrance expectations from elementary school through 12th grade, with SAT/ACT preparation and college counseling.

Small classes

More one-on-one attention, instruction tailored to each learner, and a real relationship with every teacher.

Part of the neighborhood

Students connect with West Hollywood through service projects, civic participation and partnerships with local institutions.
