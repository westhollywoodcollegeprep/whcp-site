Welcome to WHCP

A School Where Every Student Is Known

West Hollywood College Preparatory School is a private, nonprofit Pre‑school through 12th grade center of learning located in the heart of West Hollywood, California. We emphasize achievement in math, language arts, and science.

Students learn in a nurturing environment with small classes, enjoy a superb curriculum and faculty, and are encouraged to participate in civic and community life. Our intimate class sizes mean every student is seen, challenged, and supported — from their first day of pre‑school to their college acceptance letter.

Our Mission & Goals

College-Ready Curriculum

Rigorous academics aligned with university entrance expectations, from elementary school through 12th grade, with integrated SAT/ACT preparation and college counseling.

Small Class Sizes

Intentionally small classrooms mean more one-on-one attention, instruction tailored to each learner, and a genuine relationship with every teacher.

Civic Engagement

Students connect with the West Hollywood community through service projects, civic participation, and partnerships with local institutions.

Admissions

Enrollment Is Open Year-Round

In addition to the regular school year, WHCP offers Summer School, Summer Camp, and Saturday School. Spaces fill quickly — we encourage families to begin the conversation early.
